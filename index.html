The short answer: there is no recommendation, and giving one would be a fabrication. The two decisions on the table — sell before expiry, or renew — both depend on a rent, a price and a basis. None of the three exists in this workspace. What does exist is a verified picture of the city and metro economy, a verified set of tax rules, confirmed official channels for every hazard and site question, and a complete list of what is still missing. It refuses the unanswerable part

Four things would decide this file, and none of them is public. Each is a named deliverable with an owner, not a research task that can be finished by reading the web.

  • The lease: term, rent, options and notice dates → private abstract.
  • The price: a valuation and closed comparables → broker and appraiser.
  • The tax: basis and depreciation history → the owner's CPA.
  • The building: condition, insurance and hazard zones → engineer and broker.

What is deliberately absent

By instruction and by design, this public product contains no street address, no assessor parcel number, no owner or tenant identifying detail, no lease text, and no figure describing the owner's finances. People and the property appear only as OWNER-1, TENANT-A, PARCEL-A, ADVISER-1…6. Anything that could locate the parcel is on the Flagged for Review page with the reason it was withheld rather than published.
