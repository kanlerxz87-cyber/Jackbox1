The 8 blocking questions, in the order to close them

Sequence matters as much as content
OrderQuestionWhy now
1Q-02, Q-03, Q-04 — lease, options, notice calendarCheapest to close and the only one where delay is irreversible. A missed notice window cannot be recovered by analysis.
2Q-21 — the owner's objective in writingWithout it, every subsequent comparison is ranked against an unstated benchmark, and the ranking is unfalsifiable.
3Q-11, Q-12 — basis and residenceDetermines whether the after-tax module can produce a number at all. Unrecaptured section 1250 gain is taxed at up to 25% federally, so the depreciation history is not a rounding detail.
4Q-09, Q-10 — value and comparablesGives the sale case a numerator. Note that Q-05 (market rent) is equally blocking and sits with the lease work.
5Q-01 — title and vestingNeeded before anyone can sign, and it changes the tax analysis.

Questions this product cannot answer, by definition

Three questions in the register are not research problems and should not be treated as gaps in the analysis. Q-21 (the owner's objective) is a decision, not a fact. Q-22 (the access list) is an administrative act. Q-08 (whether this unit is in the closure programme) requires the counterparty to speak, and no amount of public research substitutes for asking. Everything else is obtainable.
