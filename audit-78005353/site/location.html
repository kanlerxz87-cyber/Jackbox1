The brief asks to replace city and county proxies with trade-area figures. This page does the honest version of that: it says exactly which proxy is being used, what the correct replacement is, which official dataset holds it, and the two-step private procedure that produces it without publishing the address.

Why there is no trade-area table on this page. A trade area is defined by a drive-time ring drawn around a point. The point is the parcel. Publishing the ring publishes the parcel. There is no official agency that publishes a pre-computed three-minute drive-time ring for an anonymous location, so the replacement must be computed privately and only the aggregate published.

Step 2 — the private procedure that produces the real figures

Do these in the offline workspace with the real address. Record the date and the source for each. Publish only the aggregate, and only if the aggregate cannot be reverse-engineered to a location.

  1. Resolve the address to Census geography. Use the Census Geocoder to obtain the 2020 census tract, block group and ZCTA. Official tool: Census Geocoder. The county FIPS for Los Angeles is 06037. Treat the resulting tract list as private, because it locates the parcel.
  2. Pull the same-vintage ACS tables for those tracts. Keep one vintage across every table so the comparison is internally consistent. Tables: B19013 (median household income), B01003 (total population), B25064 (median gross rent), B15003 (educational attainment), S0101 (age and sex). Reference: American Community Survey.
  3. Pull workplace employment for the ring, not residential labour force. Residence-based participation is the wrong denominator for lunch traffic. Reference: LEHD / LODES, and the Census Bureau's OnTheMap for workplace-area characteristics.
  4. Pull school performance for the assigned schools only. Use the district and school codes that the address resolves to. Official source: CAASPP Reporting, California Department of Education. The report must name the administration year.
  5. Pull planning and zoning. ZIMAS by address for zoning, General Plan designation, specific plan and overlays. Official source: City of Los Angeles Department of City Planning.
  6. Pull traffic counts. Caltrans AADT covers the State Highway System only. City street counts must be requested from the city traffic engineering or public works department. Official source: Caltrans Traffic Census Program.
  7. Pull competitor supply. Count quick-service units and approved new ones inside the ring, and mark each open, under construction or approved. Cross-check against the city's building permit records and a site visit.

What can be published once this is done

Publishable

  • Index values and percentage changes for the ring, as long as the ring is not described precisely enough to locate it.
  • The count of competing units, as a number.
  • The direction of travel: improving, flat or deteriorating, with the period.
  • Any statutory designation that applies across a whole district rather than one parcel.

Not publishable, ever

  • The address, the parcel number, the coordinates, or a map with a marker on it.
  • A ring small enough that it contains only a handful of blocks.
  • Exact tract identifiers, because a tract list in the right place is a location.
  • Anything that pairs a figure with a photograph of the storefront.

Why this is the right trade-off

The brief sets privacy as the highest priority and asks for trade-area precision at the same time. Those two goals conflict only if the precise answer is published. They do not conflict if the precise answer is computed privately and the conclusion is published in aggregate. That is what this page does: it publishes the method in full, so the work is reproducible by an authorised adviser, and it withholds the output, so the public site cannot be used to find the property.

The economic direction from verified city and metro data is genuinely useful and is on the Market & Economy page. What is missing is magnitude at the corner, and this page is honest about the fact that magnitude requires work that cannot be published.
