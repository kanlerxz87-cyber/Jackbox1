Turning rules into one number

  • The private calculator wires these rules into a single comparison: after-tax sale net proceeds versus the present value of the hold case. All dollar inputs are adviser-entered assumptions and never leave the browser.
  • California has no preferential capital-gain rate; a federal-only estimate is therefore incomplete. FTB ↗ and FTB 2025 law analysis ↗
  • Depreciation recapture at up to 25% and the 3.8% NIIT can materially change net proceeds; basis and MAGI must come from a CPA privately. IRS Topic 409 ↗ IRS Topic 559 ↗
  • Property tax reassessment on a change of ownership is governed by Proposition 13 rules; the actual bill for this parcel is unverified. BOE Publication 800-10 ↗
