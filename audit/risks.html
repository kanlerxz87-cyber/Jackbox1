Black-swan posture

  • Wildfire, earthquake and flood are officially mapped hazards; combine them with lost rent, repair costs and impaired exit liquidity in one stress run rather than three isolated estimates. FEMA NRI ↗ CAL FIRE ↗ CGS ↗
  • Insurance availability is part of the risk: the FAIR Plan is an insurer of last resort with coverage limitations and NFIP flood policies typically have a 30-day waiting period. CDI ↗ FEMA ↗
  • For wealth preservation and growth, avoid concentration in a single unverified tenant and avoid leverage before rent and hazard costs are verified; keep both transaction options open until the gates close. BLS ↗
