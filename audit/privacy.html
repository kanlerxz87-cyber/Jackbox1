Governance

Privacy & anonymisation

The highest-priority rules: placeholders only, no personal information published, no network calls and no persistence of inputs.

Personal information privacy is the highest-urgency requirement.

No personal information appears in the final website product or anywhere on the published site. Any personal information discovered during research is for internal use only, in an access-controlled offline workspace, and is represented publicly only by placeholders.
