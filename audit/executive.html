Overview

Executive summary

The important details, the current decision, and the conditions that would change it — with an official source for every factual claim.

Best decision available today (process decision).

Do not sell and do not renew yet. Keep the asset in a reversible hold, protect every notice window, and run a time-boxed verification programme that produces both an after-tax sale case and a negotiated renewal case on the same valuation date. This is not choosing a winner: it is the only choice that does not require inputs that have not been verified. A transaction winner may be selected only after Gates 1–6 close.

Why this is the answer

Evidence that exists, and its limits

  • Regional labour conditions are measurable: BLS reports LA-Long Beach-Glendale MD unemployment of 4.6% and total nonfarm employment of 4,662.8 thousand in October 2026 (preliminary, not seasonally adjusted); EDD independently reports the LA County rate at 4.6%. BLS ↗ EDD ↗
  • Demographic direction is mixed: Census QuickFacts shows LA County population down 3.2% from the 2020 estimates base to July 2025, while LA city is down 0.8%; county median household income is $90,112 and city $81,939 (2020–2024 ACS, 2024 dollars). These are city/county proxies, not trade-area figures. Census ↗
  • Issuer-level direction is negative across a multi-year series: Jack in the Box system same-store sales were -1.3% in FY2024 and -4.2% in FY2025; FY2025 had 31 openings and 86 closures; brand franchise rental revenues moved $351,283k → $347,227k → $332,735k across FY2023–FY2025. This is the franchisor, not the subject tenant or parcel. SEC 10-K ↗ SEC 8-K ↗
  • The tax mechanics are verifiable: federal net capital gain is capped at 20%, unrecaptured section 1250 gain at 25%, NIIT is 3.8%, and California taxes capital gains as ordinary income with a maximum 13.3% rate. But basis, debt and owner rates are private, so no after-tax dollar result can be published. IRS ↗ FTB ↗
  • Physical risk is real but parcel status is unknown: FEMA, CAL FIRE and the California Geological Survey publish the authoritative map frameworks; a private address lookup is required before any zone status can be stated. FEMA MSC ↗ CAL FIRE ↗ CGS ↗

Balance of wealth preservation and opportunity

Risk-balanced posture

  • Preserve optionality: an irreversible sale or a long renewal both remove an option; a reversible hold keeps both while information is gathered. The official 45-day and 180-day exchange deadlines show how quickly options can vanish once a transaction starts. IRS Form 8824 instructions ↗
  • Protect against black swans without inventing probabilities: wildfire, earthquake and flood are official, mapped hazards; stress lost rent, repairs and impaired exit liquidity together rather than one at a time. FEMA National Risk Index ↗
  • Do not personalize publicly: "the best decision for the property and myself as an individual" depends on private liquidity, concentration, tax position and risk tolerance. Those are excluded from the public site by the highest-priority privacy rule. California CCPA/CPRA ↗
