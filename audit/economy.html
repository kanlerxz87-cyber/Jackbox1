These are regional figures, not trade-area figures.

Requirement W-01 asks for city and county proxies to be replaced by trade-area figures. That cannot be done until a private parcel anchor fixes a consistent Census tract set. Every value below is explicitly labelled a proxy and is kept alongside the tract-level method on Trade area & tracts.

Findings (observations, not projections)

  • Population and jobs point in different directions: the county population estimate is -3.2% versus the 2020 estimates base while October 2026 total nonfarm employment is +0.9% year over year (preliminary). A single indicator cannot support a decision. Census ↗ BLS ↗
  • Education & health services is the largest sector in the BLS table (1,057.9 thousand jobs, +3.3% y/y), while leisure & hospitality (548.0 thousand, +2.0% y/y) is the most directly relevant to restaurant demand. Both are preliminary October 2026 figures. BLS ↗
  • Local CPI-U (1982-84=100) was 360.965 in October 2026; the BLS table left the October 12-month change cell blank and published 3.7% for September 2026. We do not fill the blank. BLS ↗
  • State sources add independent context: California DOF publishes city/county population and housing estimate series, BEA publishes county GDP and personal income, and SCAG publishes the regional growth forecast. Values for a single parcel must come from tracts, not these aggregates. DOF ↗ BEA ↗ SCAG ↗
