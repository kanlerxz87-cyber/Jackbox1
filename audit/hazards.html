Regional hazard context is not parcel status.

Requirement W-04 asks to replace regional context with the parcel's actual zone status for earthquake, fire and flood. That lookup requires the private location, so all zone-status cells are blank and flagged.

Joint stress, not single-peril guessing

  • Wildfire, earthquake and flood can interrupt rent, create repair costs and narrow the buyer pool at the same time; stress all three with lost rent and exit liquidity together, and label every scenario magnitude an assumption. FEMA NRI ↗
  • CAL FIRE notes that FHSZ maps evaluate hazard rather than risk over a 30–50 year horizon, and that the 2026 SRA update is in public comment from 2026-11-09 to 2027-01-08; record the map version at lookup. CAL FIRE ↗
  • CGS states that sellers and agents must disclose when a property lies within a mapped seismic hazard zone; that obligation should be in the private sale checklist. CGS ↗
