How to read the matrix

  • Both the sale case and the renewal case must use the same valuation date, the same verified lease term and the same tax rules; otherwise the comparison is not valid. IRS Topic 409 ↗
  • A like-kind exchange is a tax-deferral path, not a valuation path; its 45-day and 180-day deadlines are strict and California still taxes gains as ordinary income. IRS ↗ FTB ↗
  • The subject lease expiry is user-supplied as a 2030–2035 range; issuer filings do not publish a store-level expiry, so any path that depends on a date is currently unverified. SEC EDGAR ↗
