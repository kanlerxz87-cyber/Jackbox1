How to close these four blanks

Fire — CAL FIRE FHSZ

Look the APN up in the Office of the State Fire Marshal's Fire Hazard Severity Zone viewer, then confirm whether the City of Los Angeles has adopted its own Very High zone ordinance, because local ordinance governs what applies (Gov. Code §51179). Save a dated PDF or screenshot — the maps are being reissued, with a 2025 LRA rollout in progress. If the parcel lands in High or Very High, a defensible-space inspection has been required on sale since 1 July 2021.

Earthquake — CGS

Two separate checks, often confused with each other. First, Alquist-Priolo Earthquake Fault Zones (surface rupture) — the Los Angeles quadrangle maps were revised on 15 June 2017. Second, Seismic Hazard Zones (liquefaction and landslide) via EQ Zapp. A central-LA commercial parcel can be outside the first and inside the second.

Flood — FEMA NFHL

Pull the effective Flood Insurance Rate Map panel for the parcel and confirm the zone and the base flood elevation. This is a financing gate as much as a physical one: lenders require flood insurance in a Special Flood Hazard Area, and the cost lands in the NNN reconciliation.

Insurance — the real constraint

Zone status is only half the question. The other half is whether a replacement policy can be bound at all, and at what deductible. Get three quotes using the actual address and construction type before treating any hazard finding as priced (Q-12).

Black-swan exposure, stated honestly

The three events that would matter most here — a regional earthquake, a wildfire at the urban interface, and a fluvial or coastal flood — damage value through the same three channels at once: rent stops, repairs are unbudgeted, and buyers disappear. The risk register models them together for that reason, and the sell-versus-hold page refuses to publish a number until the zone status is known.
