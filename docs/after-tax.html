After-tax module — verified rules, blank owner facts

Turns the verified tax rules into one number the owner can compare with the hold case — but only after private inputs are typed into the browser calculator. This page does not compute the owner’s tax.

Ordering used in the calculator

  1. Amount realized ≈ sale price − selling costs (user entries).
  2. Adjusted basis ≈ cost basis − accumulated depreciation (user entries).
  3. Realized gain = amount realized − adjusted basis.
  4. Unrecaptured §1250 layer = min(accumulated depreciation, realized gain), federal maximum 25% (IRS Topic 409).
  5. Residual long-term gain uses the 0/15/20% band from Topic 409 and the user’s taxable-income input.
  6. NIIT 3.8% if the user says MAGI is over the statutory threshold (IRS NIIT FAQ).
  7. California: user-entered marginal rate; booklet documents 12.30% top bracket and 1% Behavioral Health Services Tax over $1,000,000 (FTB 2025 540 booklet). California generally taxes capital gain as ordinary income — confirm with the CPA; this site does not restate the entire rate schedule.
  8. §1031: if elected, only boot is treated as recognized in this simplified model (IRS like-kind tips). Debt-relief boot is not auto-calculated.
The calculator is not a substitute for Form 1040, Form 4797, Form 8824, or a California return. Cost-segregation §1245 recapture is not modeled.
