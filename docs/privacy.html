Privacy & Data Handling

Personal information privacy is the top priority. This site publishes only anonymized, aggregate, non-identifying public-source findings. Any personal information discovered during research is for internal/authorized use only and must not appear in the final website product.

Hard rule: No parcel address, APN, owner name, tenant contact, lease document, rent roll, tax return, loan document, insurance policy, account number, or other personal/financial identifier may be published on this site or committed to public-facing content. Use placeholders such as [PARCEL_ID_WITHHELD], [OWNER_ENTITY_WITHHELD], [LEASE_DOC_WITHHELD].
