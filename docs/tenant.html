A series of same-store sales, unit counts, and closures shows direction, not one quarter. These rows are Jack in the Box Inc. brand-level disclosures. They are not the subject restaurant’s sales or rent.

How to read this

  • Two fiscal years of SSS (FY 2024 −1.3%, FY 2025 −4.2%) are a short series, not a back-test.
  • Q4 FY 2025 system SSS −7.4% is worse than the year; it is still one quarter.
  • FY 2026 guidance (−1% to +1% SSS; 50–100 closures) is a company outlook, not an observation.
