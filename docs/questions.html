Questions, Information Needed, and Answers

This page lists what must be known before any sell-or-renew decision can be reasonably made. It separates current knowns, unknowns, required evidence, and discovery methods. No personal identifiers are requested on this public site; all sensitive inputs must be collected in a secure, access-controlled workflow.

Rule: A question is "answered" only when supported by verified evidence. User-provided scenario details are recorded as unverified context, not confirmed answers.
