Decision paths

There is no defensible best financial outcome for this owner until private inputs and real market quotes exist.

PathPotential benefitMaterial risks / verificationSource

Working recommendation: preserve optionality.

Commission the private lease abstract and calendar now; solicit independent quotes for an as-is short-lease sale and a negotiated renewal. Model both only when inputs are verified. This is a process recommendation, not a selection of sell or hold.

Compare on common terms

Use a consistent valuation date, discount rate, horizon and risk assumptions. Include net rent, landlord obligations, debt service, leasing costs, capital expenditures, taxes, sale costs and exit proceeds. Stress rent interruption, repair costs and impaired exit liquidity at the same time. Quotes are observations at a date, not guaranteed transactions.
