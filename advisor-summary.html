Parcel Decision Workspace — PARCEL-A

Single-tenant quick-service parcel, central Los Angeles. Triple-net lease, expiry window 2030–2035. Decision in scope: sell before expiry, renew, hold, exchange or redevelop. No personal or parcel-identifying information appears in this document.

1. Bottom line

No recommendation can be issued, and none is issued. The sell case has no price and no tax number; the renew case has no rent and no option mechanics. Six of seven decision gates are not closed. What is established is the economic direction of the city and metro from official statistics, the tenant franchisor's own audited disclosures, the federal and California tax rules that will apply to a sale, and the authoritative official channel for every remaining parcel-level question.

5. Actions requested from the adviser reading this

  1. Counsel (ADVISER-1): private lease abstract, then a dated notice calendar reconciled line by line against it. This is the only item where delay is irreversible.
  2. CPA (ADVISER-5): adjusted basis with the land and building split, accumulated depreciation, state of residence, and a 1031 feasibility view. Note that California sources the gain to the property's location and requires FTB 3840 for out-of-state replacement property.
  3. Broker (ADVISER-3): a dated opinion of value with the income approach shown, plus at least three closed comparables with the term remaining at sale, so the short-lease discount can be observed rather than assumed.
  4. Engineer (ADVISER-2) and insurance: property condition assessment with a dated capital schedule, and the declarations page with earthquake cover and deductibles.
  5. Owner (OWNER-1): one page of investment policy, and the four hazard lookups completed privately with the panel or zone identifier recorded.

6. What would change the answer

A written confirmation that the unit is outside the franchisor's closure programme, and a tenant indication of renewal at or above market, both move weight toward holding and renewing. A failed insurance renewal, a capital event due before expiry, or a comparable renewed asset trading no tighter than a short-lease one move weight toward selling earlier. None of these has been observed.
