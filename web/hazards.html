Private checks required before any commitment

  1. Run the four official parcel lookups and save dated screenshots in the access-controlled workspace.
  2. Commission engineering / geotechnical / Phase I reports privately.
  3. Obtain at least three written insurance quotes (fire, flood, earthquake, business interruption).
  4. Stress loss of rent + repairs + exit liquidity jointly under both business-as-usual and rare black-swan scenarios (see Risks).
  5. Recheck the 2026 SRA comment outcome before relying on a fire designation.
