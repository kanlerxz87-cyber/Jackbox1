Privacy

Anonymisation rules, the never-published list, and the official privacy framework used by this decision-support site.

Privacy is the highest priority. This website publishes aggregate, non-identifying findings only. Personal information discovered during research is internal-use only and must not appear in the final website product or on the website.

Never published

  • Owner or tenant personal names, contact details, signatures or personal information
  • Street address, APN, parcel ID, PIN, legal description, map sheet or precise geolocation
  • Lease files, amendments, memoranda of lease, rent rolls, notices or guarantees
  • Owner finances: basis, depreciation schedule, loan terms, tax returns, filing status or account numbers
  • Any unit-level operating data that could identify the property

Anonymisation rules actually applied

  1. The subject is described only as "one single-tenant NNN quick-service property around central Los Angeles".
  2. Placeholders are used instead of identifiers; blank cells are labelled UNVERIFIED.
  3. Only aggregate government tables and systemwide public-company filings are reproduced.
  4. The private calculator performs arithmetic in the reader's browser; there are no form submissions, cookies, analytics, localStorage writes or network calls.
  5. The secure workflow (APN/address → tract set → planning/traffic/school/competitor evidence) is described but never executed on a public page.
  6. Authorized advisers keep parcel/owner records in an access-controlled internal workspace or offline.

Official definition (for manual review)

The California Attorney General defines personal information as information that "identifies, relates to, or could reasonably be linked with you or your household", and lists rights to know, delete, correct, opt-out, limit and non-discrimination. Publicly available property records are excluded from that statutory definition, but this site applies the stricter project rule anyway.
