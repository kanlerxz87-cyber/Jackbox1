A multi-period series of same-store sales, unit counts, closures and franchise rental revenue showing direction rather than one quarter.

Direction, not one quarter

The series below uses SEC EDGAR filings: three fiscal-2025 quarters plus FY2024/FY2025 annual results. System same-store sales moved +0.4% → (4.4%) → (7.1%) across Q1–Q3 FY2025 and finished (4.2%) for FY2025 versus (1.3%) in FY2024. System restaurants fell 2,191 → 2,136; closures rose 25 → 86, including a Q3 block-closure program. Jack in the Box brand franchise rental revenues fell $347,227k → $332,735k (−4.2%), chiefly from lower percentage rent on lower sales. These are systemwide observations, not the subject unit's rent coverage; that cell remains blank.
