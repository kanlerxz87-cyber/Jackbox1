After-tax sale comparison alongside a negotiated renewal case

Sale case

  • Amount realized − adjusted basis = gain (IRS Topic 409).
  • Adjusted basis must be reduced by depreciation allowed or allowable, whichever is greater (Pub. 946).
  • Long-term brackets 0/15/20%; unrecaptured §1250 gain up to 25%; NIIT 3.8% on the lesser of NII or MAGI excess.
  • California taxes all capital gains as ordinary income; no lower state capital-gain rate.
  • Section 1031 is an alternative only for qualifying real property; boot triggers recognition.

Renew / hold case

  • Private rent and escalations (blank here) drive pre-tax NOI.
  • Rents are generally net investment income for NIIT purposes.
  • Prop 13 limits the base rate to 1% plus voter-approved bonded indebtedness, with a 2% annual assessed-value cap absent a change of ownership or new construction.
  • Negotiated renewal term, tenant improvements, credit and downtime are private inputs.

Because basis, purchase date, depreciation, loan terms, filing status, tax rate area and the renewal proposal are all unverified, no single after-tax number is published. The private calculator produces that number locally for the reader.
