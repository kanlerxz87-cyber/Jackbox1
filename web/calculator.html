Private browser-only calculator

A private calculator that runs in the reader's browser and turns verified tax rules into one number comparable with the hold case — without publishing or storing any private input.

Private by design. This calculator runs entirely in the reader's browser. It makes no network calls, sets no cookies, writes nothing to localStorage/sessionStorage and submits nothing to a server. Inputs are assumptions; official rules are listed on the After-tax module. Close the tab to discard everything.
