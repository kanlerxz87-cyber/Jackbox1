Deep research for a reliable projection system

Use SCAG Connect SoCal 2024 as the official long-range projection framework, BLS/EDD as observed labour series, DOF as observed population series, SEC filings as observed operator series, and the official hazard/insurance agencies for tail risk. A versioned, back-tested cash-flow model is deliberately not published: verified inputs and sufficient comparable history do not yet exist. When built, it must keep observations and assumptions in separate columns, pin data vintages, and re-run whenever a gate changes.

Wealth preservation & growth opportunity

For the property and the owner as an individual, the risk-balanced course is to avoid an irreversible commitment while the option value of the exact lease date, renewal proposal and quote set is unknown. Balancing means: keep liquidity and privacy (no identifying publication), preserve the renewal option by reconciling the notice calendar, avoid a forced sale into an unpriced short-lease discount, and avoid holding through an uninsured/unengineered hazard exposure. A final winner is selected privately only after the gates close.
