# Risk register and black-swan scenarios

Scenario magnitudes are assumed, never published as facts. OBSERVATION = official material; ANALYSIS = reasoning; UNVERIFIED = blank and flagged.

| ID | Decision / scope | Exposure | Potential effect | Mitigation | Evidence class | Official source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| K-01 | Sell now | Unverified pricing and tax basis | A sale can lock in a discount based on an assumed short-lease penalty and an uncomputed tax bill. | Do not commit until dated quotes and a CPA computation exist; keep both cases on the same valuation date. | ANALYSIS | https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc409 |
| K-02 | Sell now | California tax treatment | California taxes capital gains as ordinary income, so a federal-only estimate understates the bill. | Include the ordinary-rate treatment and any NIIT in the after-tax module. | OBSERVATION | https://www.ftb.ca.gov/file/personal/income-types/capital-gains-and-losses.html |
