Lease & calendar

A private lease abstract is supposed to be commissioned and reconciled with the notice calendar before any sale or renewal commitment. This page shows the abstract structure, the reconciliation attempt, and precisely why it cannot close yet.

Reconciliation result: CANNOT RECONCILE YETBoth sides of the reconciliation are incomplete. The lease side has no exact dates (NC-01 to NC-04 all null), and the calendar side is missing the insurance renewal (NC-08). A reconciliation over an eight-year date window would be false precision.

What would close the reconciliation

  • Executed lease and all amendments, read by a qualified abstractor, producing exact dates for NC-01 to NC-04.
  • Insurance binder with renewal date and premium history.
  • Any estoppel certificate already issued to a lender or prior buyer.
  • Any City of Los Angeles retrofit order or notice affecting the building.
The hard stopNo sale or renewal commitment should be signed until NC-01 and NC-02 are pinned to exact dates. An option missed is unrecoverable; everything else on this page is recoverable.

The one dated item that expires in days

Decline-in-value filing, 2026-2027 tax year. The Los Angeles County Assessor opened the window on 2026-07-02 and the deadline is 2026-11-30 — four days after this report's 2026-11-26 data cut-off.

This is the only action item on the entire site that is simultaneously verified, free, and time-limited. It reduces the property tax line, which in a triple-net hold is the largest fixed expense the owner controls. It does not require any of the missing inputs, only the assessed value on the county bill. Under BOE Pub 29 (Prop 13), a long-held parcel also enjoys a 2% annual assessment cap that resets to market value on change of ownership.

Source: Los Angeles County Assessor — https://assessor.lacounty.gov/proposition-13
