W-04 · Parcel-level hazard screening

Hazard screening

The requirement is to replace regional hazard context with the parcel's actual zone status for earthquake, fire and flood. This page delivers the machinery and the proof that it works — and refuses to claim the parcel's status until the parcel's coordinates exist.

Read this before any number belowThe three official hazard tools below were successfully queried, but only at a REFERENCE POINT (downtown Los Angeles, 34.0522 / -118.2437) because the parcel coordinates are withheld. These are therefore demonstrations that the method works, NOT the parcel's zone status. Parcel zone status stays 'unverified' until G-03 is closed in a secure workflow. Nothing here may be quoted as the parcel's hazard rating.

What the regional record already proves

Seismic: low frequency, high severity

Only four magnitude 4.5+ earthquakes occurred in the central-LA bounding box in 26 years — yet ASCE 7-22 assigns Seismic Design Category D at the reference point (SS 2.25g), and USGS UCERF3 estimates 30-year Los Angeles probabilities at 60% (M6.7), 46% (M7.0) and 31% (M7.5).

That combination is the definition of a tail risk: a model calibrated on observed frequency will understate loss almost every year, then be catastrophically wrong once.

Fire, flood & California insurance repricing

The county carried a major wildfire disaster declaration (DR-4856) in January 2025, six fire-management declarations across 14 months, and five storm and flood declarations in two winters. Meanwhile CDI Q1 2026 data shows commercial FAIR Plan policies elevated at 21,464 (up from 21,200).

These are verified federal and state records with numbers and dates. Any stress model that ignores insurance repricing or treats disasters as once-in-a-generation events is mis-calibrated.

The gap that no public source can closeBuilding-specific retrofit status, liquefaction susceptibility and — most importantly — the actual insurance binder are all private. Those three documents convert four of the black-swan scenarios on Model & stress from qualitative to quantified. Until they exist, any dollar loss figure for a seismic or fire event would be invented.

Privacy. This site publishes aggregate, non-identifying findings only. No parcel address, owner identity, APN, lease document, rent figure or personal finance appears anywhere in this repository or on this site. The parcel is referred to as PARCEL-001 throughout.

Integrity. Every data row carries a source identifier linking to an official source. Where an official source could not confirm a value, the value is left blank and listed on Flagged for review. Nothing is inferred or filled in.
