Sell versus hold

An after-tax sale comparison side by side with a negotiated renewal case, and no winner declared until verified inputs exist. Below: what each side requires, what is verified, the tax rules that apply, how the short-lease discount must be measured, and a calculator that keeps every private number inside your browser.

No winner is declared on this page — deliberatelyThe sell side is missing 10 of 10 inputs and the hold side 7 of 7. Any 'recommended' verdict produced from that would be fabricated, which the owner explicitly prohibited.

What can be said now: Three things are already decidable and do not require a winner: (1) the decline-in-value filing on NC-07 is a dated, low-cost, verified action; (2) the renewal-notice calendar must be pinned even if the sell path is chosen, because an option that lapses removes leverage from any sale; (3) §1031 must be considered before signing a sale, because it cannot be elected retroactively.

Three tax facts that change the sell decision most
  • Depreciation comes back at 25%. Unrecaptured section 1250 gain is capped at a 25% federal rate — often higher than the 20% applied to the rest of the gain. Long holds accumulate a lot of it.
  • California offers no relief. Net capital gain is taxed as ordinary income. There is no state equivalent of the federal preferential rate.
  • §1031 must be decided before signing. Forty-five days to identify, one hundred and eighty to complete. It cannot be elected retroactively.

Pricing the short-lease discount from real quotes

A short-lease discount is the gap between the cap rate a buyer pays for 15 years of remaining term and the cap rate they pay for 3-5 years. For single-tenant retail that gap is commonly several hundred basis points, but 'commonly' is not a quote. Because the discount multiplies directly into value, a 100 bp error on a 5% cap rate is roughly a 17% error in price.

Method, ready to run the moment quotes exist

  1. Take the long-term cap rate from Q-01 and the short-term cap rate from Q-02 on the same asset, same submarket, same month. Any difference in asset quality invalidates the comparison.
  2. Short-lease discount = short-term cap rate minus long-term cap rate, in basis points.
  3. Value effect = NOI / long-term cap rate minus NOI / short-term cap rate. Report both the discount in bp and the value gap in dollars; they answer different questions.
  4. Sanity check against Q-03 and Q-04. If the broker-implied discount is not corroborated by at least one closed transaction or appraisal, treat it as an opinion and label it as such on the site.
  5. Back-test: apply the implied discount to three historical local sales if any can be documented, and show the error. Until step 5 can be run, the model stays labelled 'unbacktested'.
Anti-patternDo not average Q-01 and Q-02 into a single blended cap rate. The whole point is that they differ, and that difference is the priced risk.
