W-01 · Trade-area figures

Market & trade area

The brief asks to replace city and county proxies with trade-area figures. This page does something more useful than supplying different averages: it shows, row by row, why each city-level figure fails as a trade-area proxy, and what would replace it.

Why a trade area cannot be drawn yetA trade area is a drive-time ring around the parcel. The parcel location is withheld for privacy and was never supplied in a usable form, so the ring has no centre. Additionally, the Census API rejected every keyless request, so even the tract-level building blocks could not be pulled. Both blockers are logged as G-01 and G-02 with a documented fix.

The tract pull that replaces them

Seven variables, one query

Once a Census API key exists, one request per tract in the county returns everything a drive-time ring needs:

  • B01003_001E
  • B19013_001E
  • B17001_002E
  • B25077_001E
  • B25064_001E
  • B15003_022E
  • B23025_005E

population, median household income, poverty count, median home value, median gross rent, bachelor's-or-higher count, unemployed count. These are the seven inputs the demand and land-value screens need at tract level.

Blocker and fix

Blocker: G-02 — API key required

Keyless requests returned a Missing Key page from api.census.gov.

Fix: register the free key, then run the documented endpoint. This is the cheapest open item in the whole project.

https://api.census.gov/data/2024/acs/acs5?get=...&for=tract:*&in=state:06%20county:037

Traffic and demand

Caltrans is the official source for traffic volumes on the state highway system, publishing Annual Average Daily Traffic by segment. City of Los Angeles surface streets are published by the city traffic engineering office instead. Neither can be pulled until the adjacent segment is known, which depends on the parcel location. This is logged as G-12.

Competitive supply within 1, 3 and 5 miles has no official federal publisher. It requires either a field survey of the ring or a licensed location-intelligence vendor, and any vendor figure must be labelled non-official on this site. Logged as G-11.

Privacy. This site publishes aggregate, non-identifying findings only. No parcel address, owner identity, APN, lease document, rent figure or personal finance appears anywhere in this repository or on this site. The parcel is referred to as PARCEL-001 throughout.

Integrity. Every data row carries a source identifier linking to an official source. Where an official source could not confirm a value, the value is left blank and listed on Flagged for review. Nothing is inferred or filled in.
