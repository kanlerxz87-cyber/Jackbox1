IR-01 · severity: high

The parcel is not identified anywhere in the repository or the brief

high

The brief says 'around central los Angeles' but supplies no address, APN or coordinates. Privacy rules forbid publishing them, but they are also simply absent. Every parcel-level deliverable — tract census, flood zone, fire severity zone, traffic count, competitive ring — therefore cannot be completed, only prepared.

IR-02 · severity: high

The lease expiration is stated as a five-year window (2030-2035)

high

A five-year window is not a term. A 2030 expiration with no options and a 2035 expiration with two five-year options are different assets, priced differently. No public source — including the tenant's SEC filings — discloses per-store lease expiration dates at this granularity, so the window could not be externally verified either.

Impact. The single largest driver of value in this project is unresolved. The site refuses to price the asset until it is fixed.

Action requested. Provide the executed lease or state the four calendar dates directly.

Sources: S-011

IR-03 · severity: medium

The brief mixes two different decision makers

medium

It asks for the best decision 'for the property and myself as an individual'. Those can conflict. The property-optimal answer might be 'hold for the rent', while the owner-optimal answer might be 'sell to reduce concentration'. Resolving that requires private net-worth and liquidity data that cannot appear on a public site.

Impact. The site separates the two: it can describe the property arithmetic, but the portfolio-fit judgement is flagged as an adviser conversation.

Action requested. Confirm which framing has priority, or treat both as constraints.

Sources: none available — this is a gap in the public record itself

IR-13 · severity: medium

Two different official Los Angeles population figures appear across the repo's parallel trees

medium

A parallel session recorded LA city population as 3,878,704 from Census Vintage 2024 release CB25-77. This tree records 3,869,089 from QuickFacts V2025 (a July 1, 2025 estimate). Both are official; they are different vintages and are not comparable. The same pattern applies to unemployment: 5.9% is LA County not-seasonally-adjusted for September 2025 (EDD), while 4.6% is the LA-Long Beach-Glendale metropolitan division for October 2026 (BLS).

Impact. A reader comparing the two trees could conclude one is wrong. Neither is. Every figure in this tree states its geography and period, and the Census Bureau itself warns that different vintage years are not comparable.

Action requested. When a single figure is chosen for any external use, cite the vintage with it. Do not average or blend vintages.
