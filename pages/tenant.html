Tenant & lease risk (SEC)

Jack in the Box Inc. (CIK 0000807882) is the public franchisor. In a franchised NNN pad, the direct tenant on the lease is normally a franchisee entity unless the franchisor is the sublessor or guarantor. Corporate filings establish system trajectory and franchisor credit; they do NOT disclose this specific store's sales, rent or closure risk.

What this multi-period series shows — and what it cannot showA multi-year series shows direction rather than one quarter: Jack in the Box system same-store sales moved from −1.3% (FY24) to −4.2% (FY25) and improved sequentially across FY26 (−6.7% Q1 → −3.8% Q2 → −1.1% Q3), while store closures reduced the system from 2,191 to 2,115 units and franchise rental revenues fell 4.2% in FY25 and 6.8% in 9M FY26. Meanwhile, corporate refinancing moved to 7.624% on the $500M Series 2026-1 notes and Del Taco closed its sale on 2025-12-22. None of this discloses PARCEL-001 store sales or the franchisee's balance sheet (see G-05 & G-13).
