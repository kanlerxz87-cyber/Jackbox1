Executive summary

The question. For a single-tenant Jack in the Box on a triple-net lease in central Los Angeles, with an expiration reported somewhere between 2030 and 2035: sell before expiry, or renew?

The answer today. It cannot be answered responsibly yet, and this site says so rather than manufacturing a verdict. Both the sell side and the hold side are missing every property-level input. A recommendation built on those inputs would be a fabricated cap rate attached to an eight-year date window.

What the verified evidence does say.

  • The metro labour market is sound but not accelerating: 4.6% unemployment in October 2026, total nonfarm employment up 0.9% year-over-year, with leisure and hospitality up 2.0%.
  • The metro housing index has rolled over at the margin: 4.57% annualised over the last five years, the lowest of 27 rolling windows in the sample, and 0.82% below its 2026 Q1 peak.
  • Trailing appreciation does not predict forward appreciation here. Across 7 overlapping pairs the correlation is −0.937. Extrapolating the last five years would be systematically wrong, in the optimistic direction.
  • The risk-free rate is 4.91% (2026-11-24; 4.92% on 2026-11-23) and moved 31 basis points within November 2026 alone (4.84%–5.15%). Exit cap rates are not stable.
  • The tenant franchisor (Jack in the Box Inc.) posted negative same-store sales in FY25 (−4.2%) and Q1–Q3 FY26 (−6.7%, −3.8%, −1.1%), pruned system units from 2,191 (FY24) to 2,115 (Q3 FY26, guiding ~2,100), saw 9M FY26 franchise rental revenue drop −6.8% YoY, and issued $500M of 7.624% 2026-1 Notes. See Tenant (SEC).
  • The county has absorbed a major wildfire disaster declaration, six fire-management declarations and five storm/flood declarations, while USGS UCERF3 puts 30-year Los Angeles earthquake probability at 60% (M6.7), 46% (M7.0) and 31% (M7.5). Tail risk here is demonstrated, not hypothetical.
  • California offers no preferential capital-gain rate (2026 FTB Schedules X/Y/Z run 1%–12.30% plus 1% MHSA over $1M), depreciation recapture is taxed at up to 25% federally, and the 3.8% net investment income tax may apply. The taxable sale is more expensive after tax than most owners assume.

What can be done this week without any new spending. Three things, and one has a hard deadline:

  1. Before 2026-11-30 (four days from 2026-11-26): if assessed value exceeds market value, file the decline-in-value application. It is free and the window closes in days.
  2. Pin the four lease dates — option window opens, option deadline, tenant notice, expiration. A lapsed option is the only irreversible loss in this project.
  3. Register a free Census API key so the tract-level trade-area analysis can run.

What it would take to answer the real question. Sixteen open information gates (with G-15 now closed), listed above and detailed on Open questions. One of them — the exact lease expiration and its options — matters more than every forecast on this site combined.
