4 · The finding you are being asked to test

Trailing appreciation does not predict forward appreciation in this metro: correlation −0.937 across 7 overlapping five-year pairs, with the latest trailing window the lowest of 27. Our position is that a hold case assuming continued 6–7% growth is assuming a rate the market has already stopped delivering. Please test that.
