Why the board is ordered this way

G-1 comes first because it is both the cheapest to close and the most time-sensitive. A renewal notice window that passes cannot be reopened by any amount of analysis. G-2 and G-3 come next because together they produce the only two numbers a sell-versus-renew comparison actually needs: what the asset would fetch, and what the owner would keep. G-4 sits inside both cases, because a hazard designation and an insurance file change the hold case and the buyer's view of the sale case at the same time. G-5 and G-6 are open rather than blocked: they can be closed by work already in scope, and the site is built to do the published half of G-5.

G-7 is the only closed gate. It is closed because the privacy control is verifiable in the artefact itself: you can read every page of this site and find no identifier. The remaining item under G-7 — the written access list — lives offline by necessity, which is why the gate is marked closed on the public side only.

How to move a gate

  1. Open the gate's page entry and read the outstanding requirements. Each one names the document, not a task.
  2. Check the Questions register for the matching question. It carries the owner and the source.
  3. Check Suggestions for the cheapest route to that document, with a falsifier.
  4. When the document arrives, change done to true in data/gates.json and rebuild. Do not edit the HTML.
