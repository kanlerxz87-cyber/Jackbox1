Privacy and anonymisation policy

The brief set personal-information privacy as the highest priority and highest urgency, and required that any personal information discovered during research be for internal use only and never appear in the final website product. This page states exactly how that is enforced — as a build-time check, not a promise.

Enforcement, not intent. The build script (scripts/verify.mjs) fails the site build if a US street address, latitude/longitude pair, telephone number, email address, SSN pattern, or card-number-like string appears in any published page. The site cannot be deployed with a privacy leak in it, because deployment is gated on that check passing.

The calculator's privacy design

Nothing you type leaves the browser

The sell-versus-hold calculator is the only interactive element on this site. It is engineered so that it cannot collect data even by accident:

  • No network requests. The calculator performs no fetch, XMLHttpRequest, WebSocket, or image-beacon call. It cannot transmit, because it contains no transmission code.
  • No storage. It does not touch localStorage, sessionStorage, IndexedDB, or cookies. Close the tab and the inputs are gone.
  • No analytics. There is no Google Analytics, no tag manager, no pixel, and no third-party script of any kind. The site loads exactly two local files: style.css and one JavaScript file.
  • No form action. The form's submit handler calls preventDefault(), so the browser never posts the values anywhere, including to GitHub Pages.
  • No autocomplete capture. Form autocomplete is disabled, so the browser will not offer to save the values.
  • Verifiable. Open your browser's developer tools, switch to the Network panel, and use the calculator. The only requests will be the page and its two assets.

A caveat stated honestly: GitHub Pages, as the host, sees the IP address and user-agent of any visitor to any page, as every web server does. That is outside this site's control and applies to every page here, not just this one. What is within control — the calculator's own behaviour — is designed to transmit nothing.

Why the research itself stays anonymous

The commercial reason

  • A seller whose identity and timing are public negotiates from weakness.
  • A tenant who knows the landlord is testing the market gains leverage in a renewal discussion.
  • Neighbouring owners and competing buyers can act on the information before the owner does.
  • Comparable-sale research becomes harder once the market knows a specific asset is in play.

The privacy reason

  • An address plus a tax record identifies an owner in minutes, from public data.
  • An owner's financial position is nobody else's business.
  • A tenant's lease terms and store performance are commercially sensitive to the tenant.
  • Publishing any of it would create a permanent, searchable, cached record that cannot be recalled.
The asymmetry that drives every choice here. A published page can be crawled, cached and archived within hours, and there is no reliable way to undo it. A withheld detail can always be shared privately later. Given that asymmetry, the default is always to withhold, and the published site carries only what is safe to be permanent.

Compliance notes

No personal data is processed by this site. Because the site publishes only aggregate statistics from official sources and collects nothing, it does not act as a data controller for personal information. This is a factual description of the architecture, not a legal opinion; obtain counsel's view before relying on it in any jurisdiction with specific requirements.

Official statistics are not personal data. The economic series on this site are published by government agencies in aggregate form. The Census Bureau's own documentation notes that its estimates are derived from summary tables and do not reveal the identity of any individual — the same principle this site applies.

Third parties named. Jack in the Box Inc. is named as the tenant because it is a public company whose filings are already public. No individual officer, employee, franchisee, or contact is named anywhere on this site.
