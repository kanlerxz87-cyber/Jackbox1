Pre-decision register

What must be answered before any decision is reasonable

Twenty-two questions, each marked answered, partially answered, or open. For every open question this page names who holds the answer and the specific action that discovers it — because "we need more information" is not a plan, and the value of a register is that each line can be handed to someone.

The decision-relevant count is ten, not twenty-two. The first twelve questions establish the environment. The last ten determine the outcome. A research programme that answers only the first twelve produces a well-informed owner who still cannot decide — which is the specific failure mode this register exists to prevent.

3 · The parcel — four open questions

Parcel identity and physical facts All four close with a few minutes of lookup once the address is provided.
IDQuestionWhy it decides somethingWho holds itHow to get it
Q-13 What is the assessor's parcel number (AIN), and which jurisdiction is it in? Unlocks every parcel-level record. The jurisdiction decides whether the 4.0–5.5% Measure ULA tax applies at all — a swing of up to 5.5% of the entire sale price. The owner, or a title report. Assessor Portal search by address or AIN: portal.assessor.lacounty.gov ↗. Confirm the incorporated city boundary separately.
Q-14 What is the assessed land value, improvement value, and base-year assessment? Property tax is a real cost in a NNN lease, but the recoverable amount is capped by the assessment — not by market value. A low base-year assessment under Prop 13 means low recoverable tax and a higher net cost to the landlord than a naive market-value estimate implies. County Assessor. Same portal. Request the assessment roll history, not just the current year.
Q-15 Is the parcel in a FEMA flood zone, a CGS seismic hazard zone, or a CAL FIRE Fire Hazard Severity Zone? Determines insurability, lender appetite, the buyer pool, and the loss-of-rent stress case. Can be disqualifying for some buyers outright. FEMA, the California Geological Survey, and CAL FIRE — all public. Three address lookups: FEMA MSC ↗ · CGS EQ Zapp ↗ · CAL FIRE FHSZ ↗. W-04 →
Q-16 What is the zoning, and is the drive-through a conforming or a legal non-conforming use? If non-conforming, a replacement tenant may not be able to operate a drive-through. That can reduce the site's value to land value and makes the hold-to-expiry path far riskier (R-13). The city or county planning department. A zoning verification letter, or the planning department's public zoning lookup by AIN.

4 · The lease — three open questions

These three are the highest-value gaps in the entire workspace. They cannot be answered from any public source. They require the lease document, and the lease document is private. Every valuation and timing conclusion depends on them.
Lease terms See the full 22-field abstract schema on the lease page.
IDQuestionWhy it decides somethingWho holds itHow to get it
Q-17 What is the exact expiration date, and what renewal options exist? A "2030–2035" window is a five-year ambiguity. The notice deadline for a renewal option sits 6–12 months before expiry, so the action date could be 2029 or 2034 — six years apart. It also sets the hold horizon in the calculator, which dominates the discounted value. The lease document; the owner's file; possibly the tenant. Commission a private lease abstract from counsel or a lease-administration firm. Reconcile it against the notice calendar. W-08 →
Q-18 What is the current base rent, the escalation mechanism, and any percentage-rent clause? This is the numerator of every valuation. A CPI-linked escalator tracks the verified +4.1% CPI-W; a fixed escalator does not. A percentage-rent clause means store sales affect landlord income, which in turn means store sales data is needed. The lease, plus the rent ledger. Same abstract. Also request the last three years of rent billing and any percentage-rent reports.
Q-19 Is it a true triple-net lease, and who bears rent loss after a casualty? "NNN" in a listing is a description, not a verification. If the landlord retains roof and structure, or if there is a CAM cap, or if the casualty clause lets the tenant abate rent for months, the net cash flow is materially lower than a headline NNN implies. This is also the clause that decides who absorbs R-16 and R-17. The lease. Same abstract, specifically fields B4, B5, C1, C2 and C3.

5 · The tax position — two open questions

Owner-side tax facts The rates are verified. The owner's facts are not, and they determine the number.
IDQuestionWhy it decides somethingWho holds itHow to get it
Q-20 What is the adjusted basis, acquisition date, and accumulated depreciation? Adjusted basis determines the taxable gain. Accumulated depreciation determines how much of that gain is recaptured at 25% rather than taxed at 15–20% — and recapture is not deferrable by a §1031 exchange in the same way ordinary gain is. On a long-held asset this single figure can move net proceeds by a large fraction. The owner's CPA, or the depreciation schedules in prior returns. A CPA-prepared sale projection. The worksheet calculator shows the sensitivity of net proceeds to this input.
Q-21 What is the ownership entity, the owner's filing status, MAGI, and material participation? Four separate consequences: the entity decides whether gain is taxed at individual or entity rates; filing status and MAGI decide whether the gain lands in the 15% or 20% federal tier; MAGI decides whether the 3.8% NIIT applies; and material participation decides whether NIIT applies to the rental income at all during the hold. The owner, the CPA, and the entity's operating agreement. Same CPA engagement. These are the questions an adviser will ask in the first fifteen minutes, so having them ready shortens the engagement.

6 · The valuation — one open question that gates everything

Q-22

What would a buyer actually pay today, and what would they pay for the same asset with a longer lease?

This is the question the whole exercise reduces to, and it cannot be answered from any official statistical series. No government body publishes submarket NNN retail capitalisation rates. Any number offered without transaction evidence is an opinion.

Who holds the answer:

  • Brokers with a closed-deal record in this submarket
  • Recorded deeds, which give price but usually not cap rate or term
  • Paid transaction databases, which give cap rate and sometimes term
  • Lenders, who price the term risk directly in the loan quote

What would actually close it:

  • Three to five closed single-tenant NNN trades with cap rate and remaining term
  • Three to five more with a materially longer term, for the differential
  • Two lender term-sheet quotes at 4 versus 10 years of remaining term
  • A broker's re-leasing budget if the site went vacant

With those in hand, the term coefficient in W-07 becomes estimable and the decision becomes arithmetic. Without them, it remains a judgement — and this workspace will not dress a judgement up as a measurement.

The one method with a hidden cost. Engaging a broker to research comparables is the last step, not the first, because it becomes observable: the market learns the property may be coming to sale, and that affects the price. Every other method on this list is invisible. Do them first. This is also why G5 in the decision board sits behind G3 and G4.

The minimum answer set for a reasonable decision

Not all twenty-two questions need to be answered. These nine do, and they are the ones this workspace cannot close on its own:

  1. Q-13 — parcel AIN and jurisdiction
  2. Q-15 — hazard zone status on all three maps
  3. Q-17 — exact expiry and option structure
  4. Q-18 — base rent and escalation
  5. Q-19 — true NNN status and casualty allocation
  6. Q-20 — adjusted basis and accumulated depreciation
  7. Q-21 — entity, filing status, MAGI, material participation
  8. Q-22 — comparable-sale evidence for the exit
  9. Q-16 — zoning and drive-through conformity

Six of those nine are answered by documents the owner already has or can obtain in under a week. The decision is not blocked by research difficulty. It is blocked by the fact that the private documents have not yet been provided to the analysis.
