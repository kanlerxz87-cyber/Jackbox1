W-10 · Executive summary

Advisory summary

One page, printable, written to be handed across a table. It states the question, the evidence that is settled, the evidence that is not, and the four actions that would settle it — with no identifying detail, so it can be shared before a confidentiality agreement is in place.

This page is laid out for print. Use your browser's print function (typically Ctrl+P or ⌘P) — the navigation, buttons and footer are hidden automatically and links are expanded so a printed copy remains checkable.

What this document is not. It is not a valuation, an appraisal, or a recommendation. It contains no parcel identifier and no financial figures from the owner's file, which is what makes it safe to circulate.

1 · The question

An owner holds a single-tenant, net-leased quick-service restaurant property in central Los Angeles. The lease expires within a five-year window. Three paths are available: sell before expiry, negotiate a renewal and then decide, or hold to expiry and re-let. A fourth option — deciding now on regional data alone — is rejected, because regional data cannot distinguish one block from another and the county series have just moved in a way that makes naive extrapolation misleading.

4 · The recommended sequence

  1. Identify the parcel and confirm the jurisdiction. Minutes. Unlocks four other workstreams. No commitment.
  2. Run the three official hazard lookups — FEMA flood, CGS seismic, CAL FIRE fire zone. Minutes. Can disqualify the hold path outright.
  3. Commission the lease abstract and reconcile it to a notice calendar. Days. Converts a date range into a date, and produces the hold horizon.
  4. Have the CPA produce a sale projection on filed-position inputs. Days. Turns the after-tax comparison from illustrative into decision-grade.
  5. Only then commission comparable-sales research and lender quotes. Weeks. Deliberately last, because engaging a broker is observable in the market.

5 · The trade-off, stated plainly

Wealth preservation against growth
Selling favoursHolding / renewing favours

TaxTriggers a combined rate that can exceed 35% on a low-basis asset.Defers the gain; a §1031 on a later sale can defer it again.

TimingCaptures today's price before term decays further.Renewing first captures both the term premium and the tax deferral — the only path that appears on both lists.
The one asymmetry worth carrying into the meeting. The downside of selling slightly cheap is a one-time, bounded loss. The downside of holding a single-tenant asset through a tenant default, a hazard event, or an insurance withdrawal is unbounded and correlated with the owner's other local exposure. For an owner whose wealth is concentrated in this property, that asymmetry matters more than expected-value arithmetic shows.

6 · What to ask each adviser

CPA

  • Net after-tax proceeds on a sale, on filed-position basis and depreciation
  • Does the 3.8% NIIT apply, and to the gain, the rent, or both?
  • Is a §1031 available and advisable, and what is the timing obligation?
  • How does the state surcharge above $1m of income change the number?

Lease counsel

  • Exact expiry and every renewal option, with notice windows
  • Is it a true triple net, and what is excluded from recoverables?
  • Who bears rent loss after a casualty, and for how long?
  • Is the drive-through a leasehold right or a property right?

Broker

  • Three to five closed NNN trades with cap rate and remaining term
  • What cap-rate spread does the market charge per year of remaining term?
  • What would the re-leasing cost and downtime be if the site went vacant?
  • Lender appetite at four versus ten years of remaining term
