OPT-01 1. Sell Now (As-Is Short-to-Mid Fuse, Pre-Renewal) High — eliminates single-parcel seismic/fire tail risk, tenant closure risk, and 2030–2035 vacancy cliff immediately. Moderate — redeploys net proceeds into diversified liquid or higher-growth assets, but suffers taxable drag (30%–40%+ of gain) and short-lease buyer cap-rate discount. Tax leakage (§1250 + Fed LTCG + NIIT + CA 13.3% + potential Measure ULA >$5M); buyer financing discount if <6 yrs remain; leaving upside on table if site is prime infill land. Best if store rent-to-sales is weak (>9.5%), parcel has high seismic/environmental capex exposure, or owner needs immediate liquidity. SRC-16 · Internal Revenue Service
OPT-02 2. Sell via IRC § 1031 Tax-Deferred Exchange Very High — preserves 100% of pre-tax equity (avoiding Fed/CA/recapture tax) while diversifying out of a single QSR brand and single LA fault/wildfire basin. High — swaps into 15–20 yr investment-grade NNN or multi-tenant/geographic-diversified real estate with stronger rent bumps. Strict 45-day ID / 180-day close clock can pressure overpayment for replacement property; Measure ULA (if >$5M in LA City) still applies at sale closing; QI counterparty risk. Often the strongest wealth-preservation + growth balance if basis is low (large embedded tax hit) and remaining lease term still attracts 1031/financed buyers (5–9 yrs left). SRC-19 · Internal Revenue Service
OPT-03 3. Negotiate Early 'Blend-and-Extend' Renewal Now (Hold or Sell Extended) High (if achieved on strong credit) — locks in 10–15+ yr cash-flow visibility and restores institutional buyer financing eligibility. High — cap-rate compression from extending a short lease to 15 years often creates immediate capital value exceeding TI concessions, preserving optionality to hold OR sell/1031 at peak pricing. Prematurely approaching tenant before checking store sales can invite a rent-reduction demand; franchisee may demand heavy remodel/drive-thru TI allowance. Best when PARCEL-001 has healthy store sales (<8% rent-to-sales) and strong arterial traffic, giving the tenant high switching costs. SRC-15 · U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (EDGAR)

Passive Hold into 2030–2035 Option Window (Wait-and-See) Low-to-Moderate — collects current NNN rent and keeps low Prop 13 tax base, but concentrates 100% of binary non-renewal and short-fuse illiquidity risk. Bimodal — strong ONLY if contract rent is far below market AND parcel land/re-leasing demand is exceptional; severely negative if tenant vacates a specialized QSR pad. Every passing year shrinks buyer LTV and widens exit cap rate; if tenant declines option with 6–12 months notice, landlord faces 12–24 months dark carry, taxes, insurance, vandalism, and $300k–$800k+ re-tenanting TI. Justifiable only when verified ZIMAS/infill land value or mark-to-market retail rent substantially exceeds leased-fee QSR value. SRC-14 · U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (EDGAR)
