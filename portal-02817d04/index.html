Executive Summary — How to Approach This Decision Rationally

1. Why Neither "Sell" Nor "Renew" Wins Blindly Today

Jack in the Box's systemwide franchise same-store sales contracted for four straight quarters (-4.5%, -7.2%, -7.6%, -7.0% through Q1 FY2026) under California's $20.00/hr statutory fast-food wage floor, with 86 closures in FY2025 and 50–100 guided in FY2026. Yet selling blindly can forfeit 30%–40%+ of realized gain to federal §1250/LTCG/NIIT, California's 13.3% ordinary tax, and City of LA Measure ULA (above $5M). Only parcel-specific unit sales, exact lease expiry, CPA tax basis, and real market quotes resolve that trade-off.

2. Often-Overlooked Middle Paths (Wealth + Growth)

Two structured alternatives frequently dominate a binary taxable sale vs. passive wait-and-see hold: (a) Negotiated Early "Blend-and-Extend" (extending to 12–15 years now while the tenant still values the site, compressing exit cap rates immediately) and (b) IRC § 1031 Exchange (deferring 100% of federal and California income/recapture taxes while diversifying out of a single QSR brand and single LA seismic/wildfire basin).
