Why this page matters more than the city page

The spread inside one district

Median household income across these tracts runs from $26,648 (tract 2244.20) to $145,357 (tract 2077.11) — a 5.5× range across this demonstration set. The median tract value ($46,563) sits 43% below the city median of $81,939 (INC-01) — which is precisely why a city-level average tells you almost nothing about a specific corner.

The uncertainty is large

Several tracts carry margins of error approaching or exceeding half their estimate — the widest is tract 2243.20 at $38,750 ± $36,155. Any modelling that consumes tract income must carry those MOEs, or it will produce confident nonsense. That is why the model pages show confidence labels rather than point estimates.

What would make this page real Geocode the parcel → generate 3/5/10-minute drive-time polygons → intersect with the current TIGER tract layer → pull B01003, B19013, B25077 and B23025 for exactly those tracts → recompute. That is a one-day task once the address is known.
