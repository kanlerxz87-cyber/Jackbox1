Progress is measurable, which is the point

The board above is the honest answer to “where are we?”: the analysis is largely built — the tax machinery, the tenant evidence, the regional data and the stress framework all exist and are sourced. The asset-specific facts do not. That asymmetry is worth naming, because it tells the owner exactly where the next week of effort should go: not more research, but one envelope of documents.

Day 1–2

Pull the deed and tax bill for the APN. Run the four hazard lookups. Geocode the parcel.

Day 3–5

Abstract the lease; reconcile every date to the notice calendar; confirm the tenant entity against the signature page.

Week 2

Three insurance quotes, three broker quotes, and the adviser reconstructs basis and recapture exposure.
