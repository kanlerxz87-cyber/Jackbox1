The short answer to “sell or renew?”

It cannot be answered yet, and any site that answers it for you is improvising. Two of the three decisive inputs are missing: the lease's actual expiration and renewal mechanics, and a verified short-lease discount from real quotes. The verified evidence that does exist points in a direction worth naming plainly: the tenant's Jack in the Box system has shrunk for three straight years (1,986 franchise units at FY2025 versus 2,044 at FY2023), franchise royalties are down 8.4% over two years, and the company has been selling assets and going “asset-light” — which argues that a renewal decision will be made by a franchisee operator on unit economics, not by the parent on sentiment. That is a reason to prepare the renewal case seriously and early, not a reason to sign anything today.

Where the evidence actually stands

  • Verified: the operator's own filed numbers (SEC), the legal tax machinery (California Constitution, statutes, IRS, FTB), regional labour and price data (BLS), and demographic context (Census ACS).
  • Flagged: anything regional that is standing in for a trade area; secondary reporting of a federal dataset; and figures BLS itself marks preliminary or incomplete.
  • Blank: the parcel's hazard zones, the lease terms, the assessed value and basis, and every market price. 10 rows are blank on purpose — see Flagged for review.
