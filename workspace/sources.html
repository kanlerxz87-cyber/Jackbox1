What was deliberately excluded as a source

  • Broker and listing surveys. Not official; would have been the easiest way to fill the market rows, and that is exactly why they were not used.
  • News articles and blogs. One blog figure survives only as a flagged priority signal (F-06 / HAZ-02), explicitly not a finding.
  • Private data vendors. Tract and income values were taken from Census tables via a public mirror of the Census API, and the API shape is linked so it can be re-pulled.
  • Any model or forecast. No forecast is published anywhere on this site. The calculator applies statutory rules to inputs the reader supplies.
