Property tax — the holding cost

Article XIII A. The base rate is 1%; voter-approved debt sits on top, so the effective bill is higher than 1% — which is why the calculator asks for the actual tax rate area rate rather than assuming 1%.

Transfer tax — the sale cost

Two layers: the county documentary transfer tax under RTC 11911, and the City of Los Angeles Measure ULA surtax above $5m. Measure ULA at 4–5.5% is larger than most owners expect and is often the difference between selling and holding.

Income tax on the gain

The federal and California layers that apply to an individual owner. Note the sequencing: depreciation recapture is taxed at up to 25% before the remaining gain reaches the 20% capital-gain rate, and California taxes the whole gain as ordinary income at up to 13.3%.

Depreciation — what creates the recapture

39-year straight-line on the building (land is not depreciable), mid-month convention, zero salvage. Every year of depreciation converts what would have been capital gain into unrecaptured §1250 gain at up to 25% — which is why the total tax on a sale is not simply the capital-gain rate.
