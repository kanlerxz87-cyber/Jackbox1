Tick the ones you want implemented first in the shortlist tool at the bottom; the selection is yours and nothing here has been pre-decided.

All suggestions

Fifteen suggestions, ordered by priority. Priority reflects dependency — the highest-priority items unblock the others.
IDSuggestionAreaPriorityWhat it involvesWhy it matters
S-01Build the private parcel and owner record workspace Privacy & data highest Stand up an access-controlled internal store (encrypted, single-tenant, logged) that holds the deed, APN, lease, tax bills and owner financials. Publish only aggregate summaries to the site.Nothing parcel-specific can be researched today without somewhere private to put it. This is the top blocker and it is also the top privacy control.
S-02Geocode and run the four hazard lookups in one sitting Hazard highest One afternoon: FHSZ viewer, CGS EQ Zapp, NFHL panel, ZIMAS zoning. Save dated evidence for each.Converts four blank rows into four verified rows, and unblocks insurance quotes and lender conversations.
S-03Abstract the lease and reconcile the notice calendar Lease highest Extract every date, notice window, cure period and consent requirement; check each against the calendar; have counsel sign off.The option window is the only genuinely irreversible deadline in this analysis.
S-04Commission three broker opinions and three insurance quotes Market highest Written indicative pricing on the actual asset with the actual remaining term, plus three bindable insurance quotes.Fills the two market blanks that currently block the entire sell case.
S-05Have the tax adviser reconstruct basis and recapture Tax highest Adjusted basis, depreciation history, suspended losses, entity form and the owner's marginal rate — in writing.The after-tax comparison cannot be run without it, and the calculator's output is only as good as these inputs.
S-06Replace city and county figures with drive-time tract data Trade area high Build 3/5/10-minute rings, enumerate tracts, re-pull ACS tables, recompute with margins of error carried through.Removes the largest single methodological weakness on this site.
S-07Diarise every deadline with layered reminders Lease high Calendar the option notice window at 24, 12, 6 and 3 months before, with a named owner for each.Cheap insurance against the default-to-vacancy outcome.
S-08Open a renewal conversation 24 months before expiry Lease high Approach the franchisee operator first (not the parent) with unit-level economics in hand.The company's asset-light strategy means the operator, not corporate, decides — and operators decide early.
S-09Stress-test the model, do not point-forecast it Analysis high Run a probability-weighted renewal assumption (100/50/0%) rather than a single line, and show all three.The uncertainty band on expiry (2030–2035) is itself five years wide; a single number would hide it.
S-10Model the 1031 exchange before listing, not after Tax high Identify replacement candidates and engage a qualified intermediary before a sale decision.A deferral can dominate the after-tax arithmetic and changes which option wins.
S-11Get an insurance-market read before any renovation spend Hazard medium Ask the broker what the carrier appetite is at the address before committing capital.There is no point improving an asset the market will not insure.
S-12Run a preliminary alternate-use study Exit medium Local architect: what could the site become if the tenant leaves, at what cost, with what entitlement risk?That value is the floor under the sell decision and the downside in the hold case.
S-13Publish the working file to advisers, not the public Governance medium Keep this site anonymous and share the private workspace under NDA with a named adviser list.Protects negotiating position as well as personal information.
S-14Re-verify the rate and price rows quarterly Governance medium The Treasury yield moves daily; CPI and labour series revise monthly; brackets index annually.Prevents the model from quietly drifting out of date.
S-15Record the eventual decision and its rationale here Governance low Date, choice, inputs relied on, and who advised.Turns this workspace into an audit trail for the next review or the estate.

Site improvements: how this page would make decisions better

What works now

  • Every published value carries a source link and a verification status; blanks are visible rather than hidden.
  • The tax machinery is traceable to primary law, so an adviser can check it rather than trust it.
  • The calculator runs entirely in the browser — no data leaves the reader's machine.
  • The gate board makes progress measurable instead of rhetorical.

What should be added next

  • A drive-time engine. Replace the demonstration tract set with the real ring once the parcel is known.
  • A comparables database. Recorded sales with term, price and cap rate, versioned by quarter.
  • Tract trend history. Pull two ACS vintages side by side to show direction, not just level.
  • A versioned model. Date-stamp every assumption set and keep the old ones, per the back-testing requirement.
  • An export to the adviser pack. One click from calculator inputs to a signed-off PDF.
