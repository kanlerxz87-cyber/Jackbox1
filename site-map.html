Ground rules of this site

  • Anonymity first. The parcel is PARCEL-001. No address, APN, ZIP, owner, guarantor, tenant entity, lease figure or personal finance appears anywhere in this public repository or site.
  • Sourced or blank. Every data row resolves to an official (government or Federal-Reserve-hosted) URL, or is blank and flagged unverified. Nothing is inferred, interpolated or filled from a neighbouring geography.
  • No winner yet. Sell vs hold/renew is not decided. The decision rule exists; the two decisive private inputs (lease term, arm's-length cap rate) do not.
  • Privacy scan enforced. Automated checks in every tree reject address, APN, ZIP, email, phone numbers, tax IDs or mailing ZIP codes; the calculator runs fully in the browser with no network, storage or cookies.
  • Not advice. This is an evidence workspace, not legal, tax, financial, appraisal or insurance advice. Commitments require authorised advisers and private documents.
