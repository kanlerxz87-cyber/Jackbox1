Executive summary

  • Area demand is mixed, county-wide. DOF's baseline has Los Angeles County at −1.47% from 2025 to 2035 while California is +2.23%; DOF projects the largest county K-12 enrollment drop in the state (240,800). EDD projects county jobs +8.5% and restaurant jobs +17.5% (2023 to 2033). These are county figures, not trade-area figures.
  • Treat projections as wide scenarios. DOF's 2019 vintage (as quoted) had about 10.8 million for 2030; the current baseline has 9,727,305. That is about −9.9% in five years (illustrative; see flag OL-F03).
  • Tenant brand is improving but still negative. System same-store sales ran −7.4%, −6.7%, −3.8% and −1.1% over the last four reported periods, with closures and higher bad-debt expense noted. Peers diverge: Burger King U.S. +8.4%, McDonald's U.S. −0.2%, Wendy's U.S. −5.7%. Full-year results arrive 2026-12-09.
  • Owner-level rules are official but owner facts are blank. Inherited property generally takes a value-at-death basis; the 2026 federal exclusion is $15,000,000; the California parent-child exclusion does not cover commercial property; Section 1031 is real property only; installment sales still recognize recapture in the sale year. Basis, estate and liquidity facts are private.
  • No sell-versus-renew winner. 9 of 9 decision gates are open. The lease, tenant strength, buyer quotes, after-tax figures and parcel hazards are all unverified.
  • Two dates need attention. The property-tax decline-in-value window closes Monday 2026-11-30 (applicability unknown); the tenant brand reports on 2026-12-09.
