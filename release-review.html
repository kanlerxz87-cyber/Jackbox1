Suggestions for the next review

  1. Reconcile duplicate datasets into one source register with exact series/table identifiers, vintages, periods, units, geography, margin of error and revision history. Retain provenance when retiring duplicate editions.
  2. Separate evidence status from task status: a published checklist can be complete while the underlying property answer remains unknown.
  3. Require counsel sign-off on the notice calendar before any commitment, CPA sign-off on the after-tax calculation, and written market quotes before pricing a short-lease discount.
  4. Validate calculations against adviser-approved offline cases, including zero/negative proceeds, vacancy, early termination and combined disaster stress. Do not present scenarios as probabilities.
